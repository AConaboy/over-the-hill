Frequently asked questions

FAQs

Everything you need to know about camping, facilities and what to bring for the weekend.

When you can arrive

The campsite will open at 3:00 pm on Friday. Please do not arrive earlier unless you have arranged this with one of the hosts.

Campers should leave by midday on Sunday.

What is available

  • Space for tents
  • Toilets
  • Drinking water
  • Basic washing facilities
  • Limited charging points

What to bring

  • Your tent
  • Sleeping bag and sleeping mat
  • Warm and waterproof clothing
  • A torch
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Any medication you may need

Please note:

We'll remind everyone what the weather forecast is near the time

Further notices

Even if the forecast looks good, bring warm layers and waterproof clothing. Conditions may change quickly.
